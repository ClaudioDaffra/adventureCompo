## Adventure Compo Oct ’26 Text & Non‑Text  
ProjectCD Chronicles

English Version

1 From September to the first week of October  
   Submission window for all entries.
2 Send your post to:  
   cd.commodore64@gmail.com
3 Playable format:  
   For example: Commodore .prg, .d64, or index.html, or any other playable format.
4 Description of your adventure, cover image  
   A short presentation of your work.
5 Walkthrough:  
   The first steps, just enough to show the adventure in action.
6 Optional dedicated video:  
   For those who want to showcase their adventure with a personal video.
7 At the end of the submission period:  
   All adventures will be featured in a video, with description, gameplay, and any submitted video material.
8 October gameplay month:  
   The whole month of October will be dedicated to playing the entries and leaving evaluations in the comments.
9 November:  
   Final summary video, with a Special Guest.
10 Published works:  
   All entries will be uploaded to the GitHub page 2026 session, where you can download your own work.
