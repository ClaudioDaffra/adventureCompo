1. Use key to open door.  
2. Go inside.  
3. Can now drop key.  

4. Go to great hall, take candle.  
5. Go to smoking room, take matches. Light candle.  
6. Whenever candle goes out, use matches again.  

7. Go to conservatory, take steel bar.  
8. Go to children's bedroom, take doll.  
9. Go to organ loft, take cat.  
10. Drop off doll and cat at vestibule.

12. Go to study, examine fireplace to open panel.  
13. Go through tunnel to stable.  

14. Use knife to untie Agatha. Take Agatha. Drop knife.  
15. Go to stream plant. Use bar to open boiler.  
16. Drop steel bar.  

17. Take Skye.
